Life of a Starfish

Starfish, also known as sea stars, are fascinating marine animals found in oceans around the world. They live in a variety of habitats, from shallow coastal waters to the deep sea. Starfish are recognized by their unique star-shaped bodies and their ability to regenerate lost arms.

Most starfish have five arms, although some species have many more. They move using hundreds of tiny tube feet located underneath their bodies. Starfish feed on animals such as clams, mussels, and other small marine creatures. They play an important role in maintaining healthy ocean ecosystems.

Facts About Starfish

  • Starfish are also called sea stars.
  • Most starfish have five arms.
  • Starfish can regenerate lost arms.
  • They use tiny tube feet to move.
